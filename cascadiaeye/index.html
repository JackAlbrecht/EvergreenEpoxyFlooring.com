Lifetime Workmanship Warranty

We stand behind our installation for the life of your floor. If our workmanship ever fails, we make it right — period.

  • Adhesion — no peeling or delamination from installation error
  • Full surface prep & moisture vapor barrier performed to spec
  • Backed by a licensed, bonded & insured WA contractor
Within-reason coverage, in plain English: both warranties cover the floor under normal commercial use and reasonable care. They do not cover damage from abuse or misuse, gouging or impact damage, structural substrate movement or new slab cracking, flooding or moisture events beyond the vapor barrier's rated capacity, or improper cleaning chemicals.
