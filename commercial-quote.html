The Right System for Your Space

Every facility gets the system that matches how its floors are actually used — not a one-size-fits-all coating.

Flake or Quartz + Polyaspartic

Fire & Police Stations

Apparatus bays and sally ports that shrug off hot tires, diesel, and daily washdowns. Smooth, non-slip, and sealed so fluids never stain the concrete.

Solid Color Epoxy or Quartz

Schools & Universities

Seamless floors for cafeterias, kitchens, hallways, and locker rooms. No more wax cycles — one-pass mopping, with quartz for extra slip resistance.

Solid Color + Quartz Rooms

Hospitals & Medical Clinics

Non-porous, seamless surfaces with no grout lines to harbor bacteria. Solid color in corridors, quartz in treatment areas where slip safety matters.

Solid Color + Quartz Operatories

Dental Offices

An immaculate patient-facing finish in halls and waiting areas, with quartz in operatories for constant disinfecting and sure footing.

Quartz + Integral Cove Base

Veterinary Clinics

Waterproof, claw-proof, urine-proof. The cove base curves the floor up the wall — no corners for hair, dust, or odors to live in. Safe for paws, wet or dry.

Concrete Overlay

Parking Garages

Resurface spalling, pitted decks at a fraction of replacement cost. Bonded to your existing slab and built for constant heavy traffic.

Solid Color Epoxy

Office Buildings & Lobbies

A clean, modern, seamless finish for lobbies, corridors, and back-of-house. Maintenance drops to a wet mop — one install, decades of service.
